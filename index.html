The season is short

A Christmas tree farm does its whole year in about five weeks, starting in mid-November. In those weeks, the website is the farm: families deciding where to go check dates and hours first — and a page stuck on last season loses them to the farm down the road.

Getting there

4075 Rockdale Rd, Navan, Ottawa. Navan is a short drive east of Orléans — close enough for a Saturday morning trip, rural enough to feel like one.
